About Me

I am a student entrepreneur and researcher driven by the ambition to build meaningful, real-world solutions. Growing up in a Pakistan Air Force environment and studying at Fazaia institutions instilled in me discipline, and a strong sense of responsibility. At the same time, choosing to pursue entrepreneurship and research pathways required stepping beyond conventional expectations and carving a path that is rare in my community. I am particularly motivated by transforming technical ideas into scalable, impact-driven products, bridging the gap between research and real-world application.

Beyond engineering and building ventures, I actively work on expanding my perspective through travel. I also invest in strengthening my communication and leadership skills through public speaking, Model United Nations, and STEM initiatives, as I believe strong founders must articulate vision as clearly as they build products.
